Shared green, on purpose

The roof terrace and garden double as common space — planting beds, a bench, shade. Nature access is the amenity, and it costs no floor area.

Wood and bamboo, honestly used

Structure and finishes in natural, renewable materials — warm to live in, low embodied carbon, and they age instead of dating.

Sensors that subtract

Occupancy sensing exists to remove waste, not add gadgets: lights off when rooms empty, and the dashboard shows exactly what that saved today.
